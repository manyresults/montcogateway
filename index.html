The short version

Strong foundation. The next step is a system.

Montco Gateway has momentum most chambers would envy: a fresh identity, a new market, a full calendar and a bench of marketing talent. What's missing is the machinery that turns that activity into members and partners who renew every year.

What's working

Strengths to build on

  • A clear identity. "Connecting today's leaders with tomorrow's opportunities" gives the chamber a countywide story, not a single-town one.
  • A strategic plan in motion. The board and stakeholder survey asks the right questions, including whether the chamber is "nice to belong to" or "need to belong to."
  • Real anchor partners. Banks, insurance, hospitality and the trades already invest in the chamber.
  • Programs with personality. Go-Givers, Emerging Leaders and TNT each serve a distinct need.
  • Talent in the room. The council covers design, copy, video, PR, promotional products and digital.

The question underneath

Is the challenge marketing, or capacity?

The median chamber runs on six and a half people. Montco Gateway runs 50+ events, a partner program, a Conshohocken chapter, committees, a newsletter and social channels. Every new idea competes for the same hours.

A designer adds output. A system gives time back. That's the difference between what we found and what we'd recommend.
